01 Watch criteria

What belongs on this board

Use this when a posting shows up. A match needs a lane, a language, and a workplace that fits the brief.

AI

Evaluation, training tasks, red-teaming, annotation, and support around real AI products. Prefer a written brief and paid, ongoing work.

  • AI trainer or evaluator
  • Red team and safety review
  • Annotation with a clear guide
  • Applied support in English or Slovak

Crypto

Community, research help, writing, and operations for crypto companies, including compliance-adjacent support. Payroll, not a token promise.

  • Community and customer support
  • Research or writing assistance
  • Compliance, KYC ops, or policy
  • Remote roles with a normal contract

Moderation

Trust and safety, content review, appeals, and policy work. Multilingual queues that use English or Slovak stay near the top of the list.

  • Content moderation and appeals
  • Trust and safety operations
  • Policy or quality review
  • EN / SK reviewer queues

Fit filter

  • Languages English and Slovak
  • Workplace Work from home. Hybrid in London is fine.
  • Standard Paid work with a real description.

Leave unpaid trials, and on-site roles outside London.

Paid work, a real description, and a match on at least one lane: AI, crypto, or moderation. English or Slovak. Home-based, or hybrid in London. If a posting misses that, it does not need a card.
